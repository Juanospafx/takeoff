Scope of Work

Electrical scope for 52,000 sq ft Industrial Logistics Warehouse. 30-foot clear height high-bay distribution space, 12 loading dock bays, battery charging zone for electric forklifts, administrative mezzanine offices, and secure perimeter trailer yard.

Included Items

  1. 1,200A 480V/277V 3-phase main switchgear distribution and CT metering
  2. 150kVA dry step-down transformer for 120/208V general convenience power
  3. Industrial LED high bay luminaires with heavy-duty fixture hangers
  4. Power drops to 12 dock levelers and automatic overhead sectional doors
  5. 480V 30A dedicated circuits for 8 forklift fast-chargers with safety disconnects
  6. Heavy-duty safety disconnect switches (100A, 200A, 400A)
  7. High-mast 35-foot steel yard lighting poles with high-wattage LED floodlights
  8. Overhead rigid and EMT conduit raceways in high-ceiling open bays
  9. 45-foot boom lift rental for aerial conduit and fixture installation

Excluded Items

  1. Conveyor automated sorting system VFD controllers (by material handling OEM)
  2. Forklift charging units and battery rack stands (provided by fleet vendor)
  3. Perimeter security optical CCTV cameras and card access turnstiles
